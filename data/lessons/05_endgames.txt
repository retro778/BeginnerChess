LESSON rook-endgames
TITLE Rook endgame principles
CHAPTER Endgames
SUMMARY The most common endgame.
PAGE
FEN r7/8/8/P5k1/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1
ARROW green a1a4
TEXT Rook endgames happen more often than any other endgame. Some golden rules:
TEXT |- Put your rook BEHIND passed pawns - your own and your opponent's.|- Keep your rook ACTIVE; a passive rook that only defends often loses.|- Bring your KING into the game.|- Use your rook to CUT OFF the enemy king from the action.
TEXT |Later you can learn two famous positions: the Lucena position (how to win) and the Philidor position (how to draw).
END
