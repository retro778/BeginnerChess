LESSON classical
TITLE The Classical school
CHAPTER Chess styles in history
SUMMARY Principles and small advantages.
PAGE
FEN start
PLAY d4 d5 c4 e6 Nc3 Nf6 Bg5 Be7 e3 O-O Nf3 Nbd7 Rc1 c6 Bd3
TEXT Wilhelm Steinitz, the first official World Champion (1886), showed that attacks only succeed when the position is ready for them. He taught players to collect small advantages: a better pawn structure, more space, the two bishops, a weak square in the enemy camp.
TEXT |Siegbert Tarrasch turned these ideas into clear rules: occupy the centre with pawns, develop all pieces, give your pieces freedom. Positions like this solid Queen's Gambit are typical of the CLASSICAL style.
END

LESSON hypermodern
TITLE The Hypermoderns
CHAPTER Chess styles in history
SUMMARY Control the centre from a distance.
PAGE
FEN start
PLAY d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 Nf3 O-O
ARROW green g7d4
TEXT In the 1920s the HYPERMODERN players - Aron Nimzowitsch, Richard Reti, Gyula Breyer and others - questioned the classical rules. Why occupy the centre with pawns? It can also be CONTROLLED FROM A DISTANCE, with pieces such as a fianchettoed bishop.
TEXT |In this King's Indian Defence, Black lets White build a big pawn centre and plans to attack it later with ...e5 or ...c5. Nimzowitsch also wrote about prophylaxis, blockade and flexible pawn structures - ideas every strong player uses today.
END

LESSON modern
TITLE Modern dynamic chess
CHAPTER Chess styles in history
SUMMARY Imbalance, counterplay and calculation.
PAGE
FEN start
PLAY e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 a6
TEXT After the Second World War the Soviet school, led by Mikhail Botvinnik, combined deep preparation with concrete calculation. Later champions such as Bobby Fischer and Garry Kasparov showed how powerful DYNAMIC play can be: unbalanced positions, initiative and counterplay are often worth more than a small static advantage.
TEXT |Openings like this Sicilian Najdorf, where both sides have chances, are typical of modern chess. Today computers help players to test ideas, and they have shown that many "ugly" moves are perfectly good.
PAGE
FEN start
TEXT Remember: these styles are not strict rules. Real games mix all of them - a classical opening can turn into a romantic attack, and a hypermodern setup can lead to a slow positional fight.
TEXT |The best players know all these ideas and choose what the position needs. In Beginner Chess you can meet the styles yourself: try playing against the Aggressive, Positional or Defensive computer opponents!
END
