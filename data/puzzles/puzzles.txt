# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Mate in one
PUZZLE m1-01
TITLE The back rank
CATEGORY Mate in one
LEVEL 1
FEN 6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/3R2K1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Rd8#
HINT The black king is shut in by its own pawns.
EXPLAIN Rd8 is checkmate: the king cannot escape because its own pawns block f7, g7 and h7. This is the famous back-rank mate.
END

CATEGORY Save the attacked piece
LEVEL 1
FEN r5k1/ppp2ppp/8/8/8/4n3/PP4PP/2BQ1RK1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Bxe3
HINT The knight attacks your queen and your rook. Can you capture it?
EXPLAIN 1.Bxe3! The best way to save your pieces is to capture the attacker. Now you are a whole piece up.
END

PUZZLE a-05
TITLE Bishop under attack
CATEGORY Save the attacked piece
LEVEL 2
FEN rnbqk2r/pppp1ppp/4pn2/8/1bPP4/P1N5/1P2PPPP/R1BQKBNR b KQkq - 0 4
MOVES Bxc3+
ACCEPT Be7
HINT The pawn on a3 attacks your bishop. You can take something before it is lost.
EXPLAIN 4...Bxc3+ trades the bishop for the knight and gives White doubled pawns. Retreating with Be7 is also fine. Don't play Ba5 - after b4 the bishop is chased again.
END

PUZZLE bm-02

MOVES bxa8=Q+

PUZZLE bm-03
TITLE Discovered attack
CATEGORY Find the best move
LEVEL 2
FEN 6k1/4qppp/8/8/4B3/8/5PPP/4R1K1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Bxh7+
HINT Your bishop stands between your rook and the black queen.
EXPLAIN 1.Bxh7+! is a discovered attack: the bishop gives check, and the rook on e1 now attacks the queen. After the king moves, Rxe7 wins the queen.
END

PUZZLE bm-04
TITLE Trap the bishop
CATEGORY Find the best move
LEVEL 2
FEN 6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/bPP2PPP/2K5 w - - 0 1
MOVES b3
HINT The black bishop on a2 has very few squares. Close the last door.
EXPLAIN 1.b3! traps the bishop: it cannot get out, and your king will capture it with Kb2. Taking a pawn on a2 is a famous mistake!
END

PUZZLE bm-05
TITLE Remove the defender
CATEGORY Find the best move
LEVEL 2
FEN r2q1rk1/ppp2ppp/5n2/6B1/8/3B3Q/PPP2PPP/R4RK1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Bxf6
HINT Your queen and bishop aim at h7. Which black piece defends it?
EXPLAIN 1.Bxf6! removes the knight that guards h7. If Black takes back with Qxf6 or gxf6, then Qxh7 is mate. So Black has to give up material and you win a piece.
END

TITLE Quiet castling

MOVES O-O

PUZZLE bm-07
TITLE The skewer trick
CATEGORY Find the best move
LEVEL 3
FEN R7/P3k3/8/8/8/8/r7/6K1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Rh8
ACCEPT Rg8
HINT Move your rook out of the way, but keep an eye on the seventh rank.
EXPLAIN 1.Rh8! threatens to make a queen. If 1...Rxa7, then 2.Rh7+ is a skewer: the king must move and White takes the rook. 1.Rg8 works the same way.
END

PUZZLE bm-08
TITLE Lure the queen
CATEGORY Find the best move
LEVEL 3
FEN 3q2k1/pp3p1p/1b4p1/1B1N4/8/8/PP3PPP/4R1K1 w - - 0 1
MOVES Re8+
HINT Give a check on the back rank. Who has to capture, and what happens next?
EXPLAIN 1.Re8+! The black queen must take the rook: 1...Qxe8. Now 2.Bxe8 (or the knight fork 2.Nf6+) wins the queen back, and you are a whole piece ahead.
END
