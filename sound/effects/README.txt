Beginner Chess - your own sound effects (optional)
===============================================
Beginner Chess creates all of its sounds itself, so this folder can stay empty.

To replace a sound, put a file here named after it, with the extension
.wav, .ogg or .mp3:

  move  capture  castle  promote  check  checkmate  illegal  click
  victory  defeat  draw  solved  start

Example: move.wav replaces the sound of a normal move.
Only use sounds you are allowed to share (CC0 / public domain is best),
and list them in docs/ASSETS.md.
