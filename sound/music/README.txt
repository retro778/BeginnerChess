Beginner Chess - your own music (optional)
=======================================
If this folder contains .ogg, .mp3 or .wav files, Beginner Chess plays them
one after another (when Music is switched on in Options) instead of its
own built-in background music.

Only use music you are allowed to share (CC0 / public domain is best),
and list it in docs/ASSETS.md. Do not add copyrighted music.
